CGSpace publication finder

Look up research outputs on CGSpace by a colleague's email, author name or ORCID iD. CGSpace does not store emails, so the finder works out the author name from the email (e.g. e.kihoro → “Kihoro, E”) and shows you the matching names on CGSpace to confirm.

People list

Name as it appears on CGSpace (surname first works best, e.g. “Krupnik, T”). Saved only in this browser.
